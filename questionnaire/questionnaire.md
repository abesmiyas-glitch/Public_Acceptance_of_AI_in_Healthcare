# Survey instrument (questionnaire)
 An empirical study of public acceptance of AI use by healthcare providers as a support tool. 
 
## Consent : 
Dear participant, I’m conducting this survey as part of my bachelor thesis, which examines public perceptions of the use of AI in healthcare in terms of accurancy, transparency and trust. The survey is anonymous and takes approximately 8-10 minutes to complete. No personally identifiable information will be collected. This survey is open to all adults aged 18 and above : You don’t need to be a patient or have a previous healthcare experience. Participation is entirely voluntary, and you may choose to stop completing the survey at any time before submitting your responses. The information collected will be used for academic purposes only. Thank you for taking the time to participate.
